Vision

Living society simulation.

Earth Living is a persistent Fabric-based Earth world where player cities, transport, economy and community activity make the map feel alive.

The world should grow through people.

The Earth map is the stage. Cities, businesses, jobs, tourism, transport and events are the systems that turn it into a society.

  • Earth map exploration
  • Cities and regional identity
  • Transport networks
  • Businesses and jobs
  • Dynamic economy
  • Tourism and landmarks
  • Community events
  • Future custom phone system
  • Future web dashboard
